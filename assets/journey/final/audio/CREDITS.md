# Recorded audience

`crowd-cheer.wav` contains real cheering and applause from **“Crowd.Yay.Applause.25ppl.Long.wav” by jessepash**, Freesound recording [139973](https://freesound.org/people/jessepash/sounds/139973/), released under the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The recording was retrieved from the [Tiny Chopper Raceway mirror](https://github.com/jerryjazzz/tiny-chopper-raceway/blob/290c070f594c52bafd690ac70f5dedede442d1b3/godot/sounds/139973__jessepash__crowd-yay-applause-25ppl-long.wav). That game's copy was already trimmed and volume adjusted. Its embedded WAV metadata identifies jessepash, the title, “Creative Commons 0”, and the original Freesound URL. The mirror's README and `godot/credits.txt` independently identify the same creator and CC0 dedication. Copies of that evidence and the unmodified retrieved WAV are retained in `artifacts/final-fit-assets/audio/source/`.

For Diabito Fit Arcade, this copy was gently slowed without changing pitch, raised by 10 dB, given short fades and a peak limiter, and converted to mono 22,050 Hz PCM16 WAV. Duration: 1.885805 seconds. No speech or crowd voices were synthesized. The game mixes this recording into its separately composed original podium fanfare.
